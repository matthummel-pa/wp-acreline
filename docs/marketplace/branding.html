Branding and logos

Office identity on realtor sites is a house mark, a short wordmark, forest or navy, and cream paper — not a luxury-gold skin. Acreline ships original SVG marks (GPLv2). They are not NAR, MLS, or HUD trademarks.

Canonical kit: BRAND.md in the theme repo (name, tagline, Forest palette, mark paths). Product landing: matthummel.com/projects/acreline.

How to put your office on the theme

  1. Appearance → Customize → Site Identity → Logo — PNG or SVG, about 200×48 or a square mark. The header caps height at 44px.
  2. Customize → Identity → Brand name — replaces “Acreline” in the fallback lockup and footer.
  3. Customize → Colors — Forest is the sample office; Navy and Charcoal are the other palettes offices use most.
  4. Do not edit Blade to change the logo. A child theme can restyle it; the Customizer is enough for a client handoff.

Files in this pack: Documentation/assets/acreline-mark.svg, acreline-lockup.svg. Same files in the theme at public/images/brand/.

Keep body text dark on a light page (WCAG). Do not put white type on an unshaded photo.

Type

Headings and body default to Inter (SIL OFL, Google Fonts). Customize → Typography. Serif in this documentation is Georgia for the wordmark only — the live theme stays Inter unless you change it.

Fair housing / association marks

The footer line “Equal Housing Opportunity (concept)” is a sample. It is not the official HUD logo. On a live licensed office, replace it with artwork your brokerage is allowed to use. Do not copy NAR, MLS, or Equal Housing logos from the internet into this theme.

Agent designations (ABR, CRS, GRI) and MLS/NRDS fields are text fields on the Agent metabox — type what the agent actually holds.
